Welcome to Highway Hustle!

Buckle up! Choose your ride and hit the road. Dodge the oncoming traffic, survive as long as you can, and rack up points for every car you avoid. The longer you last, the higher your score climbs — how far can you go?

How to Play

Use ← → to move left and right, and ↑ ↓ to move up and down. Avoid the cars coming toward you — one hit and it's game over. The more cars you dodge, the more points you score!
